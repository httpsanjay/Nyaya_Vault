From upload to courtroom

8 capabilities
Role-based access
Investigating officers, station heads, and admins each see only the cases and actions their role permits.
Document storage
Every file is filed against a case number, so records stay organized from the first upload.
Version control
Edits create a new version rather than overwriting the last one — nothing is ever silently lost.
OCR & text extraction
Scanned pages become searchable, readable text automatically.
Digital signatures
RSA-signed approvals bind a document to the officer who signed off on it.
Integrity verification
SHA-256 hashing flags any document that's been altered after it was signed.
Controlled sharing
Documents move between stations, lawyers, and courts only with explicit, revocable permission.
Audit trails
Every view, edit, and share is logged and timestamped for later review.
